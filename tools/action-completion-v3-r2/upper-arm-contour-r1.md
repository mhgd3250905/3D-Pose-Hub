# Upper-arm contour candidate r1

The bear-plank feedback identifies the far arm's posterior contour below the sleeve, not the elbow. Pixel queries against `bear-arm-contour-baseline-r2` at frame 60 locate shared-mesh vertices 18329/35538/35539, which originate in the current torso piece (offset 14511), not an obsolete long-arm head/neck fragment. Their skin weight is entirely `leftUpperArm`.

The torso and limbs have the same 28-point `Coach_Body` seam on each upper arm. Their captured world positions coincide exactly. This excludes an open seam and a detached fragment at those points. The original source construction fits both pieces to this inherited ring; the posterior envelope grows from about 33 mm at 100 mm below the shoulder to 56 mm at 155 mm, then declines toward the elbow. It is a continuous surface shape, not an elbow twist failure. The short-sleeve shader exposes this skin shape but does not establish a clipping bug.

`upper-arm-contour-r1.mjs` is an independent, reversible candidate. It measures actual proximal and distal surface sections 55 mm either side of the shared ring and uses them to identify excess radial convexity. It removes at most 45% of that excess, tapered by a C1 window and capped at 6 mm. The shoulder, elbow, mixed-weight vertices, hands, bones, material controls and topology remain unchanged. Both sides use exactly the same rule. Both surface copies of every seam point receive exactly the same target. Local normals are recomputed, including coincident vertices and cross-piece seam normals. No frozen source or material is replaced.

CPU evidence from the captured bear pose is `F:/PoseHub-v2-production/elbow-review-20261029/bear-arm-contour-cpu-r1.json`: 1281 left / 1296 right vertices, maximum movement 5.278 / 5.467 mm; 56 matched seam positions retain zero gap; the minimum original/candidate face-normal dot is 0.9021, with no collapsed or reversed triangles. The two queried seam vertices move about 3.11 mm and 1.84 mm. These numbers describe CPU geometry at that pose, not the runtime install result: installation computes its fixed correction in the neutral pose, and GPU capture must verify that result.

The module exposes `window.__upperArmContourR1.measure()` for actual-render capture. It returns `maxSeamGapMm`, `seamPairs`, `stress`, and `maxRestMoveMm`, throwing if the gap exceeds 0.01 mm. The underlying scene and bone transforms are never changed by measurement. `detach()` restores original position and normal buffers.

Existing `normal-sync.mjs` refreshes garment refinement, not these upper-arm buffers. Piece-normal hand switching touches wrist swaps and original-source seams, while the visible shared torso normals are independent. This static correction introduces no render hook; fresh candidate pages remain the required lifecycle. GPU still must check short/long sleeve switching and shared-ring normals if those controls are part of acceptance.

Status: CPU candidate only, unreviewed, not release eligible. A matched-camera bear-plank preview is required before adopting the component. Natural muscle volume is deliberately retained; further refinement must follow visual evidence rather than silently increasing the cap.
